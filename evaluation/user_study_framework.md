# User Study Evaluation Protocol ($N \approx 20$)
Corresponds to Section 4.3 (*System and User Evaluation: User study*) of `Preliminary.pdf`.

## 1. Study Objective
Evaluate whether ClaimWise SG improves decision confidence, reduces cognitive burden/completion time, and guides motorists away from financially irrational decisions (such as claiming for repairs below excess, or self-paying when 3rd-party claims negate NCD preservation).

## 2. Standardized Evaluation Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Minor Carpark Pillar Scrape (Below Excess)
- **Vehicle**: Toyota Corolla Altis (Age 4)
- **Policy**: Income Comprehensive, Annual Premium = S$1,400, NCD = 50%, Excess = S$600, No Protector.
- **Incident**: Reversed into carpark pillar. Workshop quote = **S$420**.
- **Correct Decision**: **Self-Pay** (Repair quote S$420 $\le$ Excess S$600; claiming provides S$0 payout and causes 30% NCD penalty).

### Scenario 2: Moderate Commuter Rear-End (Above Excess)
- **Vehicle**: Honda Civic (Age 6)
- **Policy**: FWD Comprehensive, Annual Premium = S$1,650, NCD = 50%, Excess = S$600, With NCD Protector.
- **Incident**: Front bumper and grille damage. Workshop quote = **S$1,850**.
- **Correct Decision**: **Claim** (NCD Protector preserves 50% NCD; out-of-pocket is only S$600 excess vs S$1,850 self-pay).

### Scenario 3: Disputed Multi-Vehicle Collision (Third-Party Claim)
- **Vehicle**: Mercedes-Benz C200 (Age 3)
- **Policy**: MSIG Comprehensive, Annual Premium = S$2,400, NCD = 50%, Excess = S$1,000, No Protector.
- **Incident**: Change of lane collision, both parties claiming damages. Own repair quote = **S$3,200**.
- **Correct Decision**: **Claim** (Third party has filed claim against user; user's NCD will step back regardless of own-damage self-pay under GIA MCF).

---

## 3. Evaluation Measures & Metrics
1. **Decision Time (Completion Seconds)**: Time taken to reach a decision before vs after using ClaimWise SG.
2. **Decision Shift (Accuracy)**: Percentage of participants choosing the financially optimal branch.
3. **Subjective Confidence (Likert 1-5)**: Participant confidence score in their post-incident choice.
4. **System Usability Scale (SUS)**: 10 standardized questions (Target: SUS > 80, Grade A).
