# Brief participant questionnaire

Use one questionnaire per participant. The facilitator supplies an anonymous participant ID and assigned task order. Show one allocated scenario at a time. Do not distribute the evaluator answer key or design.json.

## Introduction and agreement

This is a short simulation of a car-repair decision. You do not need to have had a real accident or to own a car. The two tasks ask you to compare modeled costs under supplied assumptions; they do not ask you to make an actual insurance decision. Participation is voluntary. Only anonymous task responses and the optional background answers below are collected. Do not enter names, contact details, income, vehicle plates or policy identifiers.

Do you agree to participate and have these responses included in aggregate study reporting? Yes / No. Stop if No.

## Four optional background questions

1. How long have you held a driving licence? No licence / Under 1 year / 1–3 years / 4–9 years / 10 or more years / Prefer not to answer.
2. How familiar are you with car-insurance concepts such as NCD and excess? 1 = not familiar, 2 = slightly familiar, 3 = moderately familiar, 4 = familiar, 5 = very familiar / Prefer not to answer.
3. How confident are you in finding and checking relevant information using internet search? 1 = not confident, 2 = slightly confident, 3 = moderately confident, 4 = confident, 5 = very confident / Prefer not to answer.
4. How confident are you in using AI tools to find and check relevant information? 1 = not confident, 2 = slightly confident, 3 = moderately confident, 4 = confident, 5 = very confident / Have not used such tools / Prefer not to answer.

These are self-ratings and licence-duration bands, not objective knowledge or skill tests. Record a skipped answer as blank. CSV codes for question 1 are no_licence, under_1, 1_to_3, 4_to_9 and 10_plus. For question 4, use not_used when appropriate.

## Task instructions for both conditions

A repair quotation is the estimated cost to repair your own car. The excess is the amount you pay toward an eligible claim. NCD is a discount applied to your insurance premium. Compare the stated three-year discounted costs under the common assumptions on the scenario card.

- Without app: decide without opening or consulting ClaimWise SG.
- With app: open the actual ClaimWise SG calculator, enter the allocated scenario, inspect the output and make your choice. Login and saving are unnecessary.
- An ordinary calculator, internet search and AI are allowed in either condition. The difference between conditions is access to ClaimWise SG. Keep the supplied scenario assumptions fixed. Record other tool use, without entering searches or chat histories.
- Each task uses a different scenario. You may not revisit a completed answer. No correct-answer feedback is supplied between tasks.

## Period 1: allocated scenario and condition

1. Which option has the lower stated three-year cost? Claim insurance / Self-pay for your own repair.
2. How confident are you in that choice? 1 = very uncertain through 5 = very confident.
3. Did you actually use ClaimWise SG for this task? Yes / No.
4. Which other tools did you use? None / Ordinary calculator / Internet search / AI / More than one category. This field is optional; a facilitator may record it.

The facilitator may record elapsed seconds from revealing the complete card until the final choice, including input, search and interpretation. Timing is optional and must not be invented.

## Period 2: the other scenario and condition

Repeat the same four questions for the assigned second task. Do not repeat the background questions.

## Two optional closing questions

1. How easy was ClaimWise SG to use? 1 = very difficult through 5 = very easy.
2. How useful was ClaimWise SG for making this comparison? 1 = not useful through 5 = very useful.

These are custom single-item ratings. They are not a shortened SUS questionnaire and must not be converted to a SUS score.
